Sensory-friendly barbering

A calmer way to get a haircut

For many autistic and neurodivergent children, a trip to the barber can be loud, bright and overwhelming. Our sensory barber room is built to make it a gentler, happier experience, for children and for parents.

Inside the sensory room

  • Ocean-themed, calming spaceA soft, calming room designed with sensory needs in mind.
  • Lava lamps and a light projectorGentle lights to watch and enjoy.
  • Sensory toysAutism-friendly toys to help children settle and play.
  • Never forced, never held downChildren lead the way, telling or showing us how they want the haircut to go.

Come and play first, free of charge

Before an appointment, children are welcome to visit the room just to play, without a haircut, so they can get to know the space and build a relationship with their barber.

For children who find haircuts very hard, the shop also runs a Desensitisation Programme, which works step by step towards the point where a haircut is possible and no longer scary.

"We let them lead us by telling or showing us the way they want the service to proceed."Quoted in Professional Beauty Ireland, February 2025

Listed on the CHO7 Children's Disability Network Teams local services directory and ESDA Ireland. Sensory haircuts currently shown for existing customers; how new families join is to be confirmed.

FAQ

Good to know

What is sensory-friendly barbering?
It's a calmer way to get a haircut for children who find the barber's hard, including autistic and neurodivergent children. The sensory room has calming lights, lava lamps and sensory toys, and the child leads the pace. Nobody is forced or held down.
Can my child just visit the room first?
Yes. Children can come to play in the room without a haircut, free of charge, to get to know the space and their barber before an appointment.
Who can book a sensory haircut?
The online booking page currently shows sensory haircuts for existing customers. Please call or email to ask how to start; details to be confirmed.
Do you cut adults' hair too?
Yes, the Den welcomes all. Gents dry cuts and skin fades are on the current booking page.
